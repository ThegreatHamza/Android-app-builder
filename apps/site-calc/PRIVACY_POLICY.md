# Privacy Policy — Site Calc

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Site Calc ("the app") is developed by HamzaApps. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Data stored on your device
Your projects, calculations, prices and settings are stored **only on your device**. We have no servers and never receive this data. You can export a backup file with *Settings → Backup → Export*; the file goes wherever you choose.

Android's device backup may include app data in your Google account backup if you have enabled it. This is controlled by Android and Google.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, users in regions that require it (such as the EEA and UK) are shown a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in *Settings → Ad privacy choices*.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app interactions with ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Purchasing Site Calc Pro removes all ads, and no ad requests are made afterwards.

## In-app purchases
The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details.

## Analytics
The app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs other than the ads SDK described above.

## Permissions
The app uses internet access only for ads and Google Play Billing. It requests no runtime permissions.

## Children
The app is a construction tool not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact
Questions: contact the developer through the email address on the app's Google Play page.
