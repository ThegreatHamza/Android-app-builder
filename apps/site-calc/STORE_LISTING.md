## Full description
Site Calc is a fast, offline construction calculator for builders, contractors and self-builders. Enter your dimensions and get the materials you need, and what they will cost, in seconds.

**8 calculators in one app**
🧱 **Concrete**: slabs, footings, square and round columns. Volume, cement bags, sand, gravel and water for 1:1.5:3, 1:2:4 and 1:3:6 mixes.
🧱 **Blocks & bricks**: blocks for any wall size, minus doors and windows, plus mortar cement and sand.
🪣 **Plaster**: cement and sand for 1:4, 1:5 or 1:6 plaster.
🔲 **Tiles**: tiles and boxes with waste allowance.
🎨 **Paint**: litres and cans for any number of coats.
🔩 **Rebar**: steel weight in kg for Ø8–Ø25 bars and how many 12 m bars to buy.
⛏️ **Excavation**: dig volume, loose soil and truck loads.
🏠 **Roofing sheets**: iron sheets for mono-pitch and gable roofs.

**Cost estimates**
Enter your local prices once (per bag, per m³, per sheet…) in any currency, and Site Calc estimates the cost of every calculation.

**Projects & quotes**
Save calculations into projects, see the total cost, and share the material list on WhatsApp or export it as CSV for Excel. Pro users get a clean PDF report for clients.

**Built for the site**
• Works 100% offline, no account
• Metric units, 25 / 42.5 / 50 kg cement bags
• Accepts both 1.5 and 1,5
• Dark mode for bright sun or night work
• Backup and restore your projects

**Site Calc Pro (one-time, no subscription):** no ads, unlimited projects, PDF reports.

*Results are estimates. Always confirm quantities with your engineer or supplier.*
