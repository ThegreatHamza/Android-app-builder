# Privacy Policy — Hifz Path

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Hifz Path ("the app") is developed by HamzaApps. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary
**The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.**

## Data stored on your device
Student names, memorization progress, revision history and settings are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. We have no servers and never receive this data.

You can export this data to a backup file with *Settings → Backup → Export*. The file goes wherever you choose, and you control it.

Android's own backup may include the app's data in your Google account backup if you have enabled device backup. This is controlled by Android and Google, not by the app.

## In-app purchases
The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play on your device whether Pro has been purchased. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Ads, analytics and tracking
The app contains **no advertising, no analytics and no tracking** SDKs.

## Permissions
The app requests no runtime permissions. Network access is used only by Google Play Billing for the optional purchase.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact
Questions: contact the developer through the email address on the app's Google Play page.
