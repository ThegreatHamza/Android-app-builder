## Full description
Hifz Path is a simple daily planner for memorizing the Holy Quran. Open it each day and it tells you exactly what to do:

📖 **New lesson (Sabaq)**: the next page to memorize, at your own pace (¼ page to 2 pages a day).
🔁 **Recent revision (Sabqi)**: pages you memorized this week, revised daily so they settle.
🕌 **Old revision (Manzil)**: every page you already know comes back on a cycle you choose. Weak pages come back sooner.

**Track by page, surah and juz**
• Colour-coded mushaf view of all 604 pages (Madani mushaf)
• Mark surahs or whole juz you already know in one tap
• Rate each recitation Weak / Good / Strong

**Stay motivated**
• Daily streak and progress card
• Percentage of the Quran, juz and surahs completed
• Estimated completion date at your current pace
• 12-week activity calendar (Pro)

**Private and offline**
• Works with no internet, ideal for madrasa and travel
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Your data stays on your phone; export a backup file any time

**Hifz Path Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription)**
• Track several students: perfect for parents and Quran teachers
• 12-week activity calendar

Start from Al-Fatiha or from Juz Amma (An-Nas first), whichever suits you or your child.
